01The setup

One PPC test cycle, measured against three targets

Luckee worked with the team through a complete cycle of PPC adjustments across AI-managed and manual campaigns.

The team set three targets for the test: 800 units per month, ACOS at or below 12%, and TACOS at or below 5%. Luckee used those targets to guide campaign changes and review each checkpoint.

Across six checkpoints, AI ACOS stayed within target five times and finished below manual ACOS four times. At the first full recovery checkpoint, the account reached 313 weekly units with 9.91% AI ACOS and 2.45% TACOS.

02AI vs manual

AI ACOS was lower than manual ACOS in four of six checkpoints

The comparison includes the stronger weeks, the account transition and the first complete recovery window.

AI ACOS stayed within the 12% target in five of the six checkpoints. It was lower than manual ACOS in four, including the low-volume repair window and the first full recovery window. Across the full period, AI remained competitive and led at four checkpoints.

The AI series also moved within a narrower range. AI ACOS varied by 5.06 percentage points, from 8.84% to 13.90%, while manual ACOS varied by 6.24 points, from 8.37% to 14.61%. That matters here because the account conditions were changing at the same time.

Volume still moved sharply, from 328 units at the earlier benchmark to 54 at the low point, then back to 313. The narrower AI ACOS range kept its efficiency swing below the manual series during that movement.

03Account changes

Store and catalog changes reshaped the account

Both campaign approaches ran through the same store transition, inventory changes and catalog constraints.

The brand moved activity to its current store and paused campaigns tied to the previous store. At the same time, inventory signals changed and coverage for primary and promotable child ASINs was incomplete.

Variation splits limited review sharing, while restricted products and irrelevant traffic affected campaign coverage. These conditions meant the next adjustments had to address structure and product eligibility as well as bids.

04The adjustments

Luckee adjusted the account with operations in the loop

The work combined a store-level campaign rebuild with tighter product coverage and traffic control.

Nine campaigns tied to the previous store were paused and rebuilt as equivalent campaigns under the current store. This kept the structure aligned with the active storefront and gave later reporting windows a cleaner baseline.

Operations confirmed the primary and promotable child ASINs and checked whether inventory could support more traffic. Luckee expanded coverage for the main-selling child ASINs, paused restricted products, added negatives to Broad and Auto campaigns, and raised bids on Product Targeting routes that were already producing sales.

Luckee kept the Broad, Auto and Product Targeting entry points that were converting, while using productive manual terms as additional evidence. The team reviewed the proposed actions before execution. When one bid-down recommendation looked too aggressive, operations revised it and the change was fed into the next review cycle.

05The recovery

AI returned below target in the next checkpoint

Volume returned above 300 units while AI ACOS moved back below target.

Weekly units rose from 54 to 313. AI ACOS fell from 13.90% to 9.91%, while manual ACOS rose from 14.04% to 14.61%. In the recovery window, AI ACOS was 4.70 percentage points lower than manual ACOS.

The rest of the account moved in the same direction. TACOS fell from 5.87% to 2.45%, and the main child ASIN returned from rank 91 to rank 32. Estimated organic orders rose from 30 to 226, with estimated organic share reaching 72.2%.

06The follow-up

The follow-up held above 300 units and below target

The account sustained the recovery as AI and manual ACOS converged.

The follow-up checkpoint closed at 311 units with 8.84% AI ACOS, 8.37% manual ACOS and 2.33% TACOS. The main child ASIN reached rank 31, while estimated organic share rose to 72.7%.

This made two consecutive checkpoints above 300 units with AI ACOS below the 12% target. After the repair checkpoint, Luckee returned AI-managed ACOS to target at the next checkpoint. Manual ACOS returned to target at the checkpoint after that.

In this test, Luckee AI brought AI ACOS back under the 12% target in the next complete reporting window, one checkpoint faster than manual management.
